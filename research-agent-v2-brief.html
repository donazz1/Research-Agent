1 · What it is

Research Agent is a private research desk in a single web page. You open it, you ask, it goes out (web + X), writes a sourced memo, and files the work. You stay in control. It does the hunting and the first draft of judgment.

2 · Two desks

Desk A

Research a company

Public companies, markets, science, papers. Modes: overview, deep dive, academic, compare. Then watch, pass, or engage. Briefs: marketing, financial, scientific, paper.

Desk B

Diligence an idea

Your product idea — paragraph, PRD, Word, Markdown, or PDF, plus spoken or typed comments. Pipeline: problem, competitors, differentiation, feasibility, cost-to-serve, verdict. Park it, keep it personal, take it commercial, or both. Briefs: marketing, technical, full, summary.

Flip the pill at the top. Company tabs (Research, Summarize, Knowledge Base, Briefs, Settings) stay for companies. Diligence swaps the bar to Diligence, Ideas, Briefs, Settings. Company junk leaves the screen. Idea junk never sits on the company desk.

3 · What you can actually do

  • Research a firm, ticker, or market question.
  • Diligence an idea: attach a PRD, talk over it, hit Dig.
  • Summarize a URL or pasted article.
  • File everything in Knowledge Base. Ideas get statuses: completed, parked, personal, commercial, both, pending.
  • Brief in the formats above — then Export (PDF, Markdown, HTML, copy) or Send (email, text, share sheet).
  • Listen — tap Read and it speaks the memo.
  • Keep working on a phone — Add to Home Screen. Same desk, your data.

6 · How you set it up — in plain English

Think of three pieces: the desk (the web page), the brain (Grok, paid to xAI), and the filing cabinet (Firebase, your own free Google project).

  1. Get the page from Azztech. Don sends you the HTML (or a private link). Bookmark it on a Mac. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. That icon is the app.
  2. Get your own Grok key. Create an account at console.x.ai, make an API key, paste it in Settings. Don never needs to see that key. Do not use his.
  3. Get your own filing cabinet. Free Google Firebase project. Paste your project ID in Settings. That is the wall: your memos never sit next to his, and he never sees yours.
  4. Do not bookmark Don’s live desk. That URL is his vault. Your copy + your key + your Firebase is the whole install.

No App Store. No server of yours to keep awake. If the page is bookmarked and the keys are in Settings, you are installed.

7 · What it costs — lay terms

Azztech: no subscription. You get the desk from Don if he offers it.

The brain (xAI / Grok 4.6): you pay xAI like a meter. Listed rate in the app: $2 per million input tokens, $6 per million output tokens. In English: a typical company memo or idea Dig is usually in the cents to a couple of dollars, not a software-license invoice. A heavy week of long diligence costs more; light use is small. You see usage on xAI’s console. Nobody here invents a monthly “plan.”

The filing cabinet (Firebase): Google’s free tier is enough for personal research for most people. You use your project so you are not on Don’s bill and not in Don’s files.

Hardware: any modern Mac, PC, or iPhone browser. Optional: a microphone for spoken comments on an idea.

8 · The wall

Keys live only on the device. Cloud files live only in the Firebase project you typed in. If two people share one page and one Firebase, they share a vault. That is why friends bring their own key and their own project. Simple rule: one person, one desk, one cabinet.
